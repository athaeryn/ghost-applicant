## Voice traits
- Tone: professional yet conversational, avoiding overly formal jargon.
- Opening: concise statement of purpose or immediate value proposition.
- Sentence length: moderate, clear sentences with occasional compound structures.
- Paragraphs: 3–4 short paragraphs per letter.
- Sign‑off: straightforward "Sincerely," followed by name.
